Before it flies

  • Stability is not checked. Model it in OpenRocket and expect to add nose weight.
  • A motor case gets hot enough to soften PLA. Print the tail in PETG or ASA and glue a cardboard motor tube into the sleeve.
  • No motor retainer or launch lugs are modelled yet. The lugs came off for the look.
  • The legs print upright with the tail, so they are weakest across their layers. A hard landing can snap one.
  • Use certified commercial motors only, and fly under your local model rocketry rules.
