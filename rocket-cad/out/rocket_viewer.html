Where it departs from the reference shape

  • The tail ends in an open 86 mm nozzle. A pointed tail would choke the fan.
  • The body is about 5.4 times as tall as it is wide. The reference is nearer 10, so this reads stubbier.
  • Six windows under the checker band feed the fan from the side, because the nose is closed.
  • The legs reach a 350 mm foot circle and hold the nozzle 100 mm off the ground.

Unresolved before a first print

  • The fan bore assumes a 98 mm housing, 80 mm long. Measure the real fan and edit two parameters.
  • Nothing stops the fan rotating in its bore yet. It needs a key or the housing's own lugs.
  • Servo mounts are not modelled, only the vane shafts and pivot bosses.
  • The ESC position under the bulkhead is a placeholder until cooling is checked.
  • The leg joints are untested. Each leg hangs on two M3 bolts through printed cheeks.
